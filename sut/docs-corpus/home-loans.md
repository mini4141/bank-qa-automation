# Home loans

## Borrowing limits
A home loan can finance up to 80 percent of the property value, so the buyer needs a deposit of at least 20 percent. The loan term is up to 30 years. The variable interest rate starts from 5.9 percent per year.

## Application steps
Submit the application with your last two payslips, three months of bank statements and the signed purchase contract. The bank orders an independent property valuation, which costs 300 dollars and is paid by the applicant. Conditional approval is usually issued within five business days.

## Fixed rate option and redraw
You may fix the interest rate for one to five years. During the fixed period extra repayments are limited to 10,000 dollars per year. A redraw facility lets you withdraw extra repayments that you have already paid on a variable rate loan.
