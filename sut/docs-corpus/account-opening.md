# Opening a savings or checking account

## Who can open an account
Anyone aged 18 or older with a valid national ID card or passport can open a savings or checking account. Applicants must live in the country and hold a residential address. Minors cannot open an account on their own, and a parent or guardian must open a junior account for them.

## Documents required
You need a government issued photo ID and one proof of address dated within the last three months, such as a utility bill. A tax number is requested but is optional for savings accounts.

## Minimum deposit and fees
The minimum opening deposit is 50 dollars for both savings and checking accounts. Savings accounts have no monthly fee. Checking accounts have a monthly fee of 3 dollars that is waived when the balance stays above 1,000 dollars.

## How long approval takes
Online applications take about 10 minutes to complete. Most accounts are approved within one business day, and you receive an email once the account number is ready.
