# Cardless ATM withdrawal

## What cardless withdrawal is
Cardless withdrawal lets you take cash from an ATM without inserting a debit card. You request the withdrawal in the mobile app, and the ATM releases the cash when you enter the one time code.

## Steps
Open the mobile app, choose Cardless cash, enter the amount and confirm with your PIN or fingerprint. The app shows a six digit code and a QR code. At the ATM select Cardless withdrawal and either scan the QR code or type the code.

## Limits and expiry
The code stays valid for 30 minutes and can be used only once. The daily cardless limit is 500 dollars per customer. Amounts must be multiples of 10 dollars. A cancelled or expired code returns the amount to your account without any fee.
