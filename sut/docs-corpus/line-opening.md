# Opening an account through LINE

## Start from the LINE chat
You can open an account without visiting a branch by adding the official bank LINE account as a friend and choosing the menu item Open account. The LINE opening is available for savings accounts only.

## Identity verification
Inside LINE you photograph your national ID card and then take a selfie so the bank can match your face with the ID photo. The verification usually finishes within five minutes. If the photo is blurry you will be asked to retake it.

## Linking and activation
After approval your new savings account is linked to your LINE user so that you can check the balance and receive transfer notifications in the chat. Activation requires you to set a six digit PIN inside the LINE chat. The first deposit can be made from any other bank by transfer using the account number shown in LINE.
