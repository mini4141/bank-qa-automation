# Personal loans

## Amount, term and rate
A personal loan can be between 1,000 and 50,000 dollars with a repayment term from 12 to 60 months. Interest rates start from 7.9 percent per year and depend on your credit profile and the chosen term.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be between 21 and 65 years old, hold an account with the bank for at least three months and have a regular income of at least 2,000 dollars per month. Eligibility is decided only by income, credit history and repayment capacity. Name, gender, nationality or religion are never used to decide eligibility.

## Repayment and early settlement
Repayments are collected monthly by direct debit from your account. You can repay the loan early at any time and the bank charges no early repayment fee. An establishment fee of 1 percent of the loan amount is deducted when the loan is paid out.
