# Transfer fees and limits

## Transfer fee
Each transfer between accounts carries a fee of 1.5 percent of the transferred amount, rounded to the nearest cent. The fee is charged to the sending account on top of the amount.

## Daily limits
The daily transfer limit is 5,000 dollars per customer. Higher limits can be requested at a branch with a signed form. Transfers are processed immediately at any time of day.

## Lost or stolen cards
If your debit card is lost or stolen, freeze it immediately in the mobile app under Card settings and call the 24 hour hotline. A replacement card is delivered within five business days and the first replacement is free.
